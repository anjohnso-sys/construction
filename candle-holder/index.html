BEFORE YOU START

    CHEAT SHEET: 1 SQUARE = 1/2 INCH
    10" = 20 squares
    3" = 6 squares
    2" = 4 squares
    1" = 2 squares
    3/4" = 1 1/2 squares
    3/8" = 3/4 square
    Tip: the left and right arrow keys also move between steps.
